Lift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Lift is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab with a personal productivity dashboard. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data We Collect

None. Lift does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The extension has no servers, no analytics, and no third-party integrations.

Data Stored Locally

All data you enter into Lift — your name, initiatives, daily focus, and leadership log entries — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

Lift requests only the permissions required to replace the Chrome new tab page. It does not access your browsing history, tabs, or any website content.

Data Deletion

You can delete all Lift data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome. This permanently clears all locally stored data. You can also export your leadership log at any time using the built-in export feature before removal.

Children's Privacy

Lift is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated date above will reflect that. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact
